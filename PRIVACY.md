# Privacy policy — Namaz Timings

*Last updated: 29 September 2026*

Namaz Timings is a prayer times app for Android. It has no accounts of its
own, no advertising and no analytics company, and no server that holds your
data.

## What stays on your phone

Your city, language, settings and every prayer you mark are stored on your
phone. Prayer times are calculated on the phone and never need the internet.

## What leaves your phone, and only when

- **Update check.** Once a day, and when you open the app, it downloads one
  small public file from GitHub that says what the latest version is, and,
  if there is one, the new version of the app. Nothing about you is sent.
- **Usage count, if switched on.** A random number the app made up on first
  run, plus the app's version. No name, location, prayer data or device
  details. It can be turned off in Settings.
- **Optional Google Drive backup — only if you sign in.** If you choose
  *Settings → Streak backup → Sign in with Google*, the prayers you have
  marked are copied to a hidden app folder in **your own** Google Drive
  (Google's `drive.appdata` permission). Only this app can open that folder;
  it cannot see any of your other Drive files, and the developer cannot see
  the backup at all. Nothing passes through any server except Google's. The
  app also reads your Google account's email address, only to show you which
  account the backup is in.

## Your choices

- Everything above except the update check is optional.
- **Turn off and delete the backup** in *Settings → Streak backup* removes the
  backup from your Drive. You can also remove the app's access at any time at
  [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone.

## Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, children included.
