Put these in any empty cell of a second sheet in the same spreadsheet.
They read the "phones" sheet the script writes to.

  Opened in the last day       =COUNTIF(phones!C2:C, ">"&(NOW()-1))
  Opened in the last 7 days    =COUNTIF(phones!C2:C, ">"&(NOW()-7))
  Still around (last 30 days)  =COUNTIF(phones!C2:C, ">"&(NOW()-30))
  Ever installed               =COUNTA(phones!A2:A)
  New in the last 7 days       =COUNTIF(phones!B2:B, ">"&(NOW()-7))
  Phones on the newest version =COUNTIF(phones!D2:D, "1.3")

A note on what these mean.

"Ever installed" only counts phones that opened the app at least once with
counting switched on. It can never go down, because an uninstall cannot be
detected -- nothing runs after the app is removed.

"Still around" is the closest honest figure for how many phones have it
installed today. A phone that was switched off for a month, or had no
internet, will drop out of it and come back when it next checks in.

"Opened in the last day" is the closest honest figure for how many people are
actually using it, and it is the one worth watching.
